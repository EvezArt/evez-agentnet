# Semantic swarm reduction

The swarm is modeled as a stream, not as a fabricated count of agents.

Each producer emits a bounded EVEZ-HSM/1 atom. The reducer:
- preserves OBSERVED/SUPPORTED evidence;
- preserves UNKNOWN/INACCESSIBLE boundaries;
- preserves CONTRADICTED signals;
- carries PROPOSED actions separately;
- chooses the next grammar mode deterministically;
- hashes the resulting human-state packet.

Reduction is communication compression, not truth voting. Ten identical guesses do not become ten times as true. Humans remain the authority for consequential decisions.
