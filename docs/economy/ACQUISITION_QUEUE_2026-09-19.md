### Acquisition rule

Do not mass-apply. Use the evidence-backed queue to prepare a small number of high-fit submissions, then require explicit human approval before each external submission.

### Revenue accounting

Observed opportunity != offer != contract != invoice != payment.

Until a payment record exists with evidence, revenue remains $0 observed.
