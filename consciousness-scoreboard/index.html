Method

  • 0 means not-found, never proven-absent.
  • No quote, no score. verify.py enforces this mechanically.
  • Snapshots are cited as snapshots with their timestamp, never as the live page.
  • Documents that could not be retrieved are listed in retrieval_failures and never scored.

Scale

  • 2 — Explicitly addressed in a binding published document, with a stated mechanism.
  • 1 — Partially addressed, or addressed only in non-binding/aspirational language.
  • 0 — NOT FOUND in any located published document. 0 is a retrieval result, never a verdict of absence.
