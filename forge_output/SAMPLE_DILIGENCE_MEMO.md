**Engagement:** Pre-investment falsification of core technical claim.
**Claim under test (composite of a real pitch class):** "Our room-temperature
solid-state device extracts usable net energy from zero-point vacuum
fluctuations; scaling to 1 kW units within 18 months."
**Tier:** Fundamental-physics claim with direct valuation impact.

## Test 1 — Conservation-law audit
The claimed mechanism (Casimir-cavity differential pressure driving a cyclic
motor) reduces to extracting work from a symmetric vacuum state. In any
unitary quantum theory, the Casimir force is conservative: net work over a
closed cycle is identically zero. The pitch's energy diagram shows a closed
loop with nonzero circulation — that requires a non-conservative field the
device does not possess. **FAIL.**

## Test 2 — Thermodynamic bookkeeping
Claimed 40 W output with no fuel input and no measurable cooling of the
device body. A closed device emitting 40 W must deplete an internal free-energy
reservoir at ≥40 W. At their stated device mass, that reservoir would be
exhausted in under an hour. Their demo runs 6+ hours. **FAIL.**

## Test 3 — Scaling claim
18 months to 1 kW implies an energy-density trajectory ~10^6× beyond the
fastest validated solid-state chemistry programs of the last decade, with no
identified mechanism step. **FAIL.**

## VERDICT: FALSIFIED
Three independent falsification channels, no assumptions about intent required.
A fund holding this memo before the wire saves the entire check.
