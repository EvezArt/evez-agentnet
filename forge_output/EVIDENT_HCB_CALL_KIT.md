## WHAT SHE'LL ASK, AND THE HONEST ANSWERS

**"What is EVIDENT?"** Tamper-evident records for local government. The same
integrity architecture that banks and courts rely on — a hash-chain ledger with
sealed records — built so a small city can run it, not just a bank.

**"Does it actually work?"** The machinery runs today, at scale. The parent
stack's spine holds 11,957 hash-chained events — every seal append-only, every
artifact SHA-256 manifested, with a witness step that records who did what and
when. Tamper detection is a one-command check: `sha256sum -c`. (Verified live
2026-10-21 — this number is real and re-verifiable on request.)
