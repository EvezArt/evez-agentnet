## 1. TARGET MARKET IDENTIFICATION

**Sector A — Quantum Computing Hardware & Error-Correction Startups (Series A–C)**
~$2–4B/yr private capital flowing into superconducting/trapped-ion/neutral-atom platforms. These companies employ physics PhDs but almost nobody who can independently audit whether the *foundations* of their error-correction or readout claims hold. Their boards and investors cannot check the physics. That is the blind spot you sell into.

**Sector B — Deep-Tech Venture Capital & Quantum-Focused Funds**
Funds (e.g., quantum-vertical VCs, sovereign deep-tech vehicles) deploying $5–50M checks into physics companies with zero in-house capability to falsify technical claims. Their diligence stack is: team pedigree + competitor benchmark + one friendly academic. A single missed conservation-law violation or an impossible scaling claim costs them the whole check. You are cheap insurance at 0.1–1% of check size.

**Sector C — Aerospace / Satellite Relativistic Systems & PNT (Positioning-Navigation-Timing)**
Relativistic corrections are not academic here — GPS-grade clocks, interplanetary navigation, LEO constellation timing, and emerging cislunar PNT all live or die on GR/SR corrections done right. Contractors (and defense primes' innovation arms) ship systems where a subtle frame-dragging or Sagnac-sign error propagates into meters of navigation drift. Very few people alive can audit that math at contract speed.

*(Reserve: post-quantum cryptography firms — QCD rarely matters to them, but quantum-adjacent credibility does. Secondary market, lower ticket.)*
