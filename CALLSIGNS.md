## The reinforcements (2026-10-04)

Three vessels joined because measurement, not ambition, asked for them:

- **WITNESS** — the swarm was blind. The sensing substrate sat at `clients: 0`,
  `source: simulated`; nobody could report a room changing state. WITNESS holds
  the beat for presence, radio, breathing, departure.
- **BEACON** — the swarm was unaddressable. A hundred published pages with no
  index, no sitemap entry, no social preview. BEACON holds discoverability,
  canon, and the coordinates of everything shipped.
- **ARCHIVIST** — the swarm could prove its past but not rebuild its future from
  it. ARCHIVIST holds lineage, generation blocks, cold start, and the root
  commitment.

The Eighth still signs nothing. Eleven vessels serve; the root commitment still
transfers to no one.
